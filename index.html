01 — About

A little about me

I've spent the last few years moving between two things that don't usually share a sentence: production infrastructure and Bharatanatyam. I build data pipelines and APIs at BlackRock that have to hold up across 200+ enterprise clients — and I've trained and performed classical Indian dance for 14 years. Both taught me the same lesson, honestly: the unglamorous, repetitive part is where the skill lives.

That's probably why my favorite project isn't the one with the best accuracy number — it's the one where I had no dataset at all. At Intel, I wanted to generate Indian classical music with a model, and nothing like that existed, so I recorded and converted audio into a 343KB MIDI dataset myself before I could even start training GPT-2 on it. I'd rather build the thing that doesn't exist yet than fine-tune the thing that does.

I also co-authored a paper on video recommendation for fashion (accepted at RCAAI 2024) and ran IEEE-CIS as General Secretary — more on that, and on what I do when I'm not working, a couple of sections down.
